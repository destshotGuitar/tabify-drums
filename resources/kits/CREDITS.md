The Muldjord Kit has no rimshot, hi-hat pedal or semi-open, splash, cowbell or
clap; those articulations play the GM Rock Kit samples.

When releasing music made with the MuldjordKit samples, DrumGizmo asks for the
credit "Drum samples provided by DrumGizmo.org" where album credits exist.
